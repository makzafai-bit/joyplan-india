Privacy Policy

Last updated: 4 November 2026

This policy explains how Joyplan India ("we", "us") handles the personal details you share through the demo booking, Masterclass registration and partner application forms on joyplanindia.com.

What we collect

  • The details you enter in a form, such as your name, mobile number, email, city, profession, company and the answers you choose.
  • How you reached the page, such as the ad campaign or link you clicked (UTM parameters).
  • Basic technical information, such as your IP address and browser type, to prevent spam.

How we use it

  • To respond to your request: confirm your demo slot, share Masterclass batch details, or review your partner application.
  • To contact you on WhatsApp, by phone or by email about that request, when you have given consent in the form.
  • To measure how our advertising performs. These pages may use the Meta Pixel and Google Analytics, which set cookies and record visits and form submissions.

Sharing

We do not sell your personal data. We share it only with service providers who help us run our website, communication and advertising, and when the law requires it.

How long we keep it

We keep your details for as long as needed to handle your request and follow up on it, and then delete them.

Your choices

You can ask us to access, correct or delete your details, or withdraw your consent to be contacted, at any time. Withdrawing consent does not affect contact made before you withdrew it.
