FAQs

Software questions

Which devices does JoyPlan run on?

JoyPlan is available on the App Store and Google Play, and work can be shared across phone, tablet, laptop and desktop.

Do I need design experience?

No. Short, searchable help videos cover each tool, and a free demo walks you through your own project.

Can I export to CAD?

Yes. JoyPlan exports a CAD drawing set in one click, along with area inventories, professional drawings and estimate reports.

Does it support multi-storey buildings?

Yes. Multi-level floor plans keep every floor of a duplex or villa in one aligned project.
